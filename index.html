Math Community is a hub for collaboratively understanding and rewriting results generated by private-sector frontier AI models

Humans currently face the challenge of making sense of an expanding set of AI-generated mathematical proofs. Independent of the labs producing these proofs, we work on three things: understanding what the write-ups contain, placing the results in the existing literature, and, where they hold up, seeing them absorbed into the peer-reviewed literature through new papers by human authors.

Our first effort focuses on OpenAI’s write-ups. They are often difficult to follow, and they can miss relevant citations. We’ve forked OpenAI’s repo, and anyone can submit issues or pull requests with revisions:

Curators

Invitations in progress

We are inviting curators to manage revisions of some write-ups. In general, a curator has expertise in relevant mathematical subfields. They consider proposed edits and maintain notes and links to relevant work. Curators are encouraged to lead channels in our Slack enabling discussion with interested community members.

Accepting an edit is not peer review, and curating a write-up does not make the curator its author.

Contributing

Two kinds of contributions

1

Small, local corrections and additions to the write-ups

Corrections within scope include supplying missing references, clarifying definitions, explaining a difficult step, or identifying and repairing an error. Changes are credited and recorded, and community-edited versions are clearly marked.

2

Independently written expositions

These should be written as separate works with their own authors, who are encouraged to post them to preprint servers and submit to journals for peer review. The project repository links to these contributions and explains their relationship to the source paper.

All contributors are responsible for their own claims and should disclose substantial use of AI or other mathematical software.

To take part, open an issue or pull request on GitHub, or write to us at mathcommunityadmin@gmail.com.

Ethics statement

Independence

Active repository administrators and active curators are not employees of frontier AI labs. By “frontier AI lab” we mean an organization spending at scale to push the frontier of AI-driven mathematics; which organizations qualify may change over time. If an active admin or curator chooses to become a frontier AI lab employee, they agree to resign from their position in Math Community.
